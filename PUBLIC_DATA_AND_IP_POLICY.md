# Public Data, IP, and Privacy Policy

This repository is intentionally designed as a **public professional portfolio**, not as a raw research archive.

## Included

- Publicly reportable aggregate findings
- Method descriptions
- Recreated figures from summary statistics
- Synthetic datasets and demonstrations
- Publication and preregistration links
- Public-safe architecture and validation patterns
- High-level descriptions of R&D systems and study design

## Excluded

- Protected health information or direct identifiers
- Participant-level restricted research data
- Raw clinic exports or partner-owned data
- Confidential technical feasibility reports
- Proprietary payer criteria or contractual material
- Production application source code
- Security-sensitive architecture details, credentials, secrets, keys, or tokens
- Trade-secret algorithms or unpublished patent-sensitive implementation details
- Third-party copyrighted stimulus sets or images not licensed for redistribution

## Clinical AI demonstrations

All code in `examples/` uses synthetic data. Synthetic demonstrations are designed to illustrate analytical reasoning such as class imbalance, temporal validation, calibration, workflow burden, and data-quality checks. Synthetic metrics should never be interpreted as Hidalga clinical-study results.

## Published figures

Where a journal figure may have redistribution restrictions, this repository uses either a link to the publication or a new visualization recreated from reported summary statistics. Recreated visuals are labeled accordingly.
