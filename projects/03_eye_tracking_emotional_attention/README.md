# Eye Tracking: Emotional Attention and Cross-Modal Distraction

**Evidence status:** PhD dissertation, OSF preregistered  
**Institution:** University of Arkansas, 2025  
**Preregistration:** https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/WK4M8

## Executive readout

**Problem:** Reaction time alone cannot distinguish early visual orienting, sustained gaze, oculomotor behavior, blinking, and autonomic pupil responses.  
**Approach:** Build a 360-trial emotional dot-probe experiment synchronized with 1000 Hz EyeLink recording and three auditory conditions.  
**Result:** Angry faces delayed initial fixation relative to happy faces; smartphone notifications increased pupil variability relative to silence; several other endpoints were null or context dependent.

## Study scale

| Measure | Value |
