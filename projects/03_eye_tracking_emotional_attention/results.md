# Results and Interpretation

## Early visual orienting

Initial fixation occurred later for angry than happy faces:

- Angry raw mean: **739.94 ms**
- Happy raw mean: **702.28 ms**
- `t(35) = 3.64, p = .003`

## Pupil dynamics

Notification trials showed greater pupil variability than silence:

- Smartphone: **83.67**
- Silence: **79.80**
- `t(35) = 2.99, p = .015`

## Interpretation boundary

Pupil variability is treated as a condition-dependent physiological measure, not a direct readout of a single latent construct such as arousal or cognitive load. Several saccadic, blink, and behavioral endpoints were null or context dependent, supporting a multimodal interpretation rather than a single-metric story.
