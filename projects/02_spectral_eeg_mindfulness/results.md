# Results and Interpretation

## Supported pattern

- Smartphone notifications were associated with lower theta-band power and higher alpha- and beta-band power on target trials.
- Selected theta/TBR contrasts suggested stronger task-related differentiation in the mindfulness group, particularly for smartphone conditions.

## Critical constraint

The broad **group x sound TBR interaction was not statistically significant**. Therefore, selected follow-up contrasts should not be converted into a general claim that a brief mindfulness induction improves cognitive control under smartphone distraction.

## What this demonstrates

- Frequency-domain EEG analysis
- Randomized experimental manipulation
- Artifact/source-separation discipline
- Multiple-comparison and interaction interpretation
- Willingness to preserve a null primary interaction even when some follow-up contrasts are favorable
