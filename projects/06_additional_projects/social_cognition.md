# Social Cognition and Diversity Ideologies

## Role
Research Assistant, Social Psychology Laboratory, University of Arkansas, 2016-2018.

## Research focus
Cognition, persuasion, personality, social belief systems, and diversity ideologies.

## Methods and responsibilities
- Designed and executed laboratory and field studies
- Built and deployed survey instruments using Qualtrics
- Managed participant recruitment and protocol adherence
- Conducted statistical modeling of relationships among personality, cognitive styles, perspective taking, and social attitudes
- Communicated findings at regional and national research conferences

## KSA demonstrated
- Survey and psychometric research
- Lab and field-study execution
- Participant operations
- Behavioral data analysis
- Scientific presentation

## Selected scholarly output
Research on openness to experience, perspective taking, and support for multiculturalism was presented at the Society for Personality and Social Psychology and received an Outstanding Undergraduate Poster Award at the Arkansas Psychological Association meeting.
