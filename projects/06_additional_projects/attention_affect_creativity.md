# Attention, Affect, and Creativity: From Mindfulness to Mind Wandering

**Status:** Coauthored scholarly chapter, *The Cambridge Handbook of Creativity and Emotions* (2023)

This chapter synthesized research on focused, diffuse, and flexible attention; affective state; mindfulness; mind wandering; and creative cognition.

## Portfolio relevance

The work demonstrates the ability to synthesize heterogeneous behavioral and neuroscience literatures into a conceptual model understandable across disciplinary boundaries. That skill is directly transferable to R&D strategy, evidence reviews, product requirements, and executive scientific communication.
