# iCreate: Social Media Use, Divergent Thinking, and Creative Achievement

**Status:** First-author, peer-reviewed  
**Publication:** *Translational Issues in Psychological Science* (2022)  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1037/tps0000306

## Study design

- Final sample: **372** participants
- Alternate Uses Task for divergent thinking
- SemDis semantic-distance scoring for originality
- Creative Achievement Questionnaire for real-world creative accomplishment
- Active and passive social-media measures
- Smartphone Addiction Scale

## Results pattern

Greater divergent-thinking originality was associated with **less active social-media use** and **lower smartphone-addiction scores**, while real-world creative achievement was associated with **more active social-media use**. Passive use was not associated with either form of creativity.

## What this adds to the portfolio

- Large-sample behavioral data
- Construct operationalization
- Semantic-distance scoring
- Psychometrics
- Distinction between creative cognition and real-world achievement
- Explicit correlational limitation rather than causal overreach
