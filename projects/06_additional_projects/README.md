# Additional Projects

These projects broaden the portfolio beyond the five flagship studies. They are included to show range in behavioral science, affective research, theory synthesis, collaborative research, and early laboratory training.

| Project | Role / status | Methods or topic | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| [iCreate](icreate.md) | First author, peer reviewed | Alternate Uses Task, SemDis, CAQ, social-media measures | Large-sample behavioral data, psychometrics, semantic scoring, correlational restraint |
| [Imagination and anxiety during COVID-19](imagination_anxiety.md) | Coauthor, peer reviewed | Two-sample individual-differences analysis, anxiety, loneliness, imagination | Replication thinking, interaction models, affective science |
| [Attention, affect, and creativity](attention_affect_creativity.md) | Coauthor, scholarly chapter | Literature synthesis | Theory integration across attention, mindfulness, mind wandering, affect, creativity |
| Mindfulness and healthier food choices | Coauthor, peer reviewed | Brief body-scan intervention | Collaborative intervention research; publication-level scholarly contribution |
| Developmental cognitive-affective psychophysiology | Research assistant | Net Station EEG, emotion regulation, executive control | Early EEG acquisition, QA, artifact processing, protocol execution |
| Social psychology | Research assistant | Lab/field studies, surveys, cognition and persuasion | Behavioral study execution, recruitment, structured survey data, statistical analysis |
| Anxiety and substance-use research | Research assistant | NIH-supported behavioral research, survey/qualitative data | Clinical-behavioral research exposure, confidentiality, coding and analysis |
