# Results and Interpretation

## Right anterior insula: anticipation of large loss vs neutral

- Screen time: beta = **-0.0322**, p = **.012**
- Caregiver warmth: p = **.896**
- Screen time x warmth: beta = **0.0000150**, p = **.047**
- Adjusted R2 = **0.0073**

## Right nucleus accumbens: anticipation of large vs small loss

- Screen time: p = **.091**
- Screen time x warmth: p = **.079**
- Adjusted R2 approximately **0.0002**

## Interpretation

The anterior-insula interaction was statistically significant, but the model explained very little variance. The NAcc interaction did not meet the conventional significance threshold. This project is presented as evidence of neuroimaging-analysis breadth and effect-size discipline, not as a definitive claim about screen time causing altered brain development.
