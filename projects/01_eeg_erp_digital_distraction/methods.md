# Methods

## Experimental logic

The local-global Navon task created frequent, rare, and no-target visual conditions while auditory notification and control sounds were paired with trials. The oddball contrast separated expected from infrequent target processing.

## Outcomes

- Reaction time
- Accuracy / error rate
- P2 amplitude
- N2 amplitude
- P3 amplitude
- Oddball contrasts
- Moderation by smartphone-addiction proneness

## EEG quality control

- ERP data were excluded for technical problems or uncorrectable artifact contamination greater than 25% of trials.
- ICA and ICLabel were used in artifact-correction workflows.
- Final ERP sample: 54 participants.

## Why the method matters

The design distinguishes behavioral slowing from stage-specific electrophysiological changes. It also makes null results informative: a sound effect on N2 with no P3 effect argues against a uniform change across all measured processing stages.
