# Reproducibility and Public Materials

The publication reports the experimental design, preprocessing, and statistical analyses. A public research repository is available at:

https://github.com/jupshaw/SMARTPHONES-AND-COGNITIVE-CONTROL

This portfolio repository does not duplicate participant-level research data. The synthetic EEG demonstration in [`../../examples/eeg_qc_demo.py`](../../examples/eeg_qc_demo.py) illustrates quality-control and spectral concepts without reproducing the original analysis pipeline.
