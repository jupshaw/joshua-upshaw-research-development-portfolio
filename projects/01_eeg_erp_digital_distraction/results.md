# Results and Interpretation

## Behavioral

Across 37,769 reported trial observations, rare targets were slower than frequent targets, demonstrating the expected oddball effect. Smartphone-sound trials were slightly slower than control-sound trials:

- Smartphone: **432.97 ms**
- Control: **429.70 ms**

The effect was statistically detectable but small.

## ERP

Reported mean amplitudes included:

| ERP | Smartphone | Control | Test |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| P2 at Fz | 8.44 uV | 8.89 uV | p = .053 |
| N2 at F3 | 3.94 uV | 4.39 uV | t(53) = -2.09, p = .041 |
| P3 at Pz | 11.42 uV | 11.39 uV | p = .927 |

## Constraint on interpretation

Smartphone-addiction proneness did not consistently moderate the behavioral or neural sound effects. Multiple effects were small or null. The appropriate conclusion is therefore selective perturbation of measured processing, not a generalized claim that notifications broadly impair cognition.
