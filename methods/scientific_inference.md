# Scientific Inference and Claims Calibration

## Questions used before making a claim

1. What exactly was observed?
2. Was the analysis prespecified, exploratory, or post hoc?
3. Is the result an association, prediction, simulation, intervention effect, or causal estimate?
4. How large is the effect, not just how small is the p-value?
5. Did the result persist under relevant sensitivity, subgroup, or temporal checks?
6. Could measurement quality, selection, leakage, or missingness explain the finding?
7. What decision does the evidence actually justify?
8. What new data would change the conclusion?

## Why this matters

The same statistical output can be scientifically useful and operationally unsafe. Claims calibration is the bridge between analysis and responsible R&D decision-making.
