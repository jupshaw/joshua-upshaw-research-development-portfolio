# Research Governance, Reproducibility, and Traceability

## Traceability model

```text
claim -> requirement -> implementation -> code/configuration -> dataset ->
test -> model/version -> result -> provenance -> limitation -> release decision
```

## Research-governance practices

- IRB and human-subjects procedures
- informed consent and records-based research controls
- de-identification and minimum-necessary processing
- source lineage and provenance
- versioned datasets and models
- explicit acceptance criteria
- reproducible analysis documents
- technical claims review
- separation of retrospective, simulated, proposed, and prospective evidence

## Professional training

CITI Good Clinical Practice, Biomedical Human Subjects Research, HIPAA/Health Privacy, Data or Laboratory Specimens, and Responsible Conduct of Research.
